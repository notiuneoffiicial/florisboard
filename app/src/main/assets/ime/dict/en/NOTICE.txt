words.txt: English word frequencies, blended from Peter Norvig's count_1w
(an excerpt of the Google Web Trillion Word Corpus, norvig.com/ngrams) and
the OpenSubtitles-derived FrequencyWords en_50k list
(github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords, CC-BY-SA 4.0).
bigrams.txt: for the 16k most common words, their 12 most common
followers, from Norvig's count_2w (same source). Trimmed and lowercased;
bare contractions ("dont") were folded into their spelled forms ("don't").
